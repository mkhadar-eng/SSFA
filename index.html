A home ground for young footballers

Soccer Stars Football Academy started as a small Saturday-morning training group and has grown into one of the most active grassroots programmes in the area. We work with children and teenagers who want somewhere to learn the game properly — not just kick a ball around, but understand it, respect it, and get better at it week by week.

Every session is built around the basics: good habits on the ball, honest effort, and looking out for your teammates. Coaches track progress individually, so a seven-year-old just learning to trap a pass and a fifteen-year-old preparing for trials both get the attention they need.

Our Mission

To give every child, regardless of background, access to structured football coaching and a supportive team environment.

Our Vision

An academy known across Uganda for producing disciplined, skilful players and confident young people.
